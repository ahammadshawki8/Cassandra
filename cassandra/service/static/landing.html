The problem

Spreadsheets are the largest untested codebase on earth.

They allocate budgets, price deals, and set national policy. Almost none of them are under test, and a wrong formula looks exactly like a right one.

94%
of spreadsheets contain at least one error
Meta analysis across seven field audits
95%
of financial models audited had major errors
KPMG survey; 75% had significant accounting errors
86%
of people made an error, while estimating their own odds at 10 to 18%
Panko, controlled experiments

That last gap is the real problem. The errors are everywhere, they are consequential, and the people who wrote them are confident they are not there. Reinhart and Rogoff shaped global austerity policy off a summation range that omitted five rows. JPMorgan's London Whale loss involved a copy and paste error inside a $6.5B hole.

How it works

It does not warn you. It fixes it, then proves the fix.

Every existing tool emits a warning and asks a human to judge it. Cassandra writes the correction, applies it to a copy, recalculates the entire workbook, and refuses to show you anything that did not survive.

1 · Map
Parse into a formula dependency graph. No model calls: a parser does this better.
2 · Hunt
Five deterministic detectors plus a semantic agent that reads labels against formulas.
3 · Judge
Defect, or a legitimate modelling choice? Series seeds and conventions are dismissed.
4 · Reject
A correction that fails recalculation goes back with the reason. Three attempts, then a human.
5 · Prove
Moved exactly as predicted, nothing else touched. Only then does it reach you.

Nobody has to remember to run it

A workbook landing in a Cloud Storage bucket starts the audit. The human bottleneck is the actual root cause, so it is removed entirely.

It proves defects that are not wrong yet

A hardcoded rate equal to the assumption it replaced computes the right answer today. Cassandra proves it by perturbing the driver and showing the two workbooks diverge.

It ranks by money, not by rule

Findings are ordered by blast radius through the dependency graph, weighted toward the figures a person actually quotes in a meeting.

You get the corrected file

Every verified correction applied, anything quarantined left alone, and the original never touched. A repair, not a report.

Three ways in

Drop an .xlsx, paste a Google Sheets link, or let a bucket event start it. The Sheets path needs no OAuth, so nobody grants access to a whole Drive to audit one file.

The agents hold no tools

Not one can read a file, change a workbook, or reach the network. A prompt injected into a cell label can at worst cause a wrong judgement, which recalculation then rejects.

Architecture

Three questions go to Gemini. Everything else is code.

Detection, the dependency graph, region clustering, and every verification are plain Python. The model is consulted only where a parser genuinely cannot answer, and the Verifier that decides whether it was right asks the model nothing at all.

Research

Standing on a decade of literature, not claiming to be first.

Spreadsheet error detection is a mature field. Cassandra's contribution is the composition: autonomous, continuous, self verifying, and regression aware.

CUSTODES: Automatic Spreadsheet Cell Clustering and Smell Detection
Establishes that defective cells surface as outliers within clusters formed by tabulation style. The region clustering and Pattern Breaker implement this directly.
ICSE 2016
ExceLint: Automatically Finding Spreadsheet Formula Errors
Reference point for structural anomaly detection across a grid.
arXiv 1901.11100
SpreadsheetLLM: Encoding Spreadsheets for Large Language Models
Microsoft Research on compressing grids for language models without losing structure.
arXiv 2407.09025
Benchmark Dataset Generation and Evaluation for Excel Formula Repair with LLMs
Establishes verification of model written repairs against a calculation engine as sound methodology. Cassandra's Verifier is this idea taken as the system's only authority.
arXiv 2508.11715
Panko, What We Know About Spreadsheet Errors
Source of the prevalence figures, and of the taxonomy the six defect classes mirror.
